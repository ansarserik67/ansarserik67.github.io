FULL DOSSIER · BUCKY & AKNUR

I. SUBJECT — JAMES BUCHANAN BARNES

Born 1917, Brooklyn. Sergeant, 107th Infantry. Presumed KIA 1945. Recovered by Hydra, rebuilt as the Winter Soldier, deployed for 50+ years, freed by Steve Rogers, rehabilitated in Wakanda as the White Wolf.

Active threat classification: retired. Only remaining active assignment is the one he gave himself.

II. SUBJECT — SERIK AKNUR

Born 9 November 2005 in Uralsk, Kazakhstan. Designated THE ANCHOR. Cross-referenced in all recorded branches of the timeline as the constant variable in Barnes' psychological stability index.

Officially: no file. Functionally: the entire reason the file has a happy ending.

III. BOND ANALYSIS

Where the wipe took everything else, her name survived 25 memory suppression cycles. Hydra's own analysts logged it as an "unexplained residual" and quietly gave up on erasing it.

Assessment: bond is not memory-dependent. It is structural.

IV. STANDING ORDER

Protect Serik Aknur at all costs. Everything else — the shield, the arm, the century, the war — is negotiable.

V. CLOSING NOTE
